Common Questions

FAQs for New Residents

How do I get a Hobart address? ›
Contact City Hall at (580) 726-3325 for address assignment questions. The Kiowa County Assessor's office at the courthouse can also help confirm property addresses.
Where do I register my vehicle in Oklahoma? ›
Visit the Kiowa County Tag Agency at (580) 726-3021. You'll need your vehicle title, proof of insurance, and payment for registration fees. Oklahoma requires registration within 30 days of establishing residency.
What's the nearest hospital? ›
Elkview General Hospital is at 429 W Elm St in Hobart — (580) 726-3229 — with a 24/7 emergency room. Larger facilities are in Lawton (~65 miles) and Oklahoma City (~120 miles).
How do I enroll my child in Hobart Public Schools? ›
Contact Hobart Public Schools at (580) 726-3341 or visit hobartschools.net. You'll typically need proof of residency, immunization records, and prior school records.
What's the best way to stay connected to what's happening? ›
Follow the Hobart Chamber of Commerce on Facebook, subscribe to our community calendar at hobartchamber.org, and sign up for event notifications on the site. The Chamber is your hub for everything happening in Hobart and Kiowa County.
